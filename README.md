# Data-Analytics-Portfolio

A collection of my data analytics, finance, and business analysis projects using Python, Excel, and data visualization.

## Projects

### 1. FY2026 U.S. Federal Awards Portfolio Analysis

This project analyzes FY2026 federal contract and financial assistance award data from USAspending, focusing on the U.S. Department of Transportation, Department of Education, and Department of Housing and Urban Development.

I used Python and pandas to clean, combine, and analyze the award data. The analysis examines differences across federal agencies, award categories, recipients, programs, and recipient states.

The final portfolio contains 59,258 FY2026 awards representing approximately $112.09 billion in cumulative obligations associated with those awards.

**Tools:** Python, pandas, Matplotlib, Jupyter Notebook, Excel

**Key analysis:**
- Federal awards by agency
- Contracts vs. financial assistance
- Geographic distribution by recipient state
- Largest award recipients
- Major assistance programs and contract activities
- Data cleaning and missing-value analysis

**Data Source:** USAspending Prime Award Summary data

> Note: FY2026 runs from October 1, 2025 through September 30, 2026. The source data was downloaded before the end of the fiscal year, so the analysis represents FY2026 year-to-date data.


### 2. Ittonken Restaurant Financial & Operational Performance Analysis

This is an independent Excel project analyzing the financial and operational performance of a local restaurant in Champaign, Illinois.

I cleaned and organized POS transaction data and used Excel to convert transaction-level records into daily and monthly performance measures. I then combined sales information with cost estimates to evaluate revenue, gross margin, operating performance, order volume, and customer spending patterns.

The project also includes a dashboard designed to summarize major financial and operational indicators and identify patterns that may help explain changes in restaurant performance.

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, PivotTables, Excel formulas, financial analysis, data visualization

**Key analysis:**
- Revenue and order trends
- Average order value
- Average daily sales
- Cost of goods sold and gross margin
- Prime cost and operating profit
- Monthly and weekday performance
- Restaurant performance dashboard
- Analysis of patterns related to the UIUC academic calendar
